FINOVA is a financial planning support platform designed strictly for educational and informational purposes. All calculations, projection charts, and scenario outcomes depend on user-provided information and stated assumptions. Results are estimates, not guarantees, certified financial audits, or personalized investment advice. FINOVA does not guarantee yields or recommend purchasing or selling specific securities or assets.

How to use FINOVA

Create an account

  1. Open the person icon in the top-right corner.
  2. Choose Create account and enter your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  3. Confirm your password and register.

Log in and log out

  1. Choose Log in from the account menu and enter your registered email and password.
  2. Open the account menu to see your account name and choose Log out when finished.

Reset password

Choose Forgot password and submit your registered email. Email delivery must be configured by the site operator before reset links can be sent.

Light and dark mode

Click the moon icon to switch to dark mode; it becomes a sun. Click the sun to return to light mode.
